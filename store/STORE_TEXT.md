## Full Description (ready to paste)

The appstore page shows the text as is: no Markdown (`**`), emoji bullets instead, and no `&` (it renders oddly).

```text
Kiddo brings the playful Fitbit Kiddo watchface (Design and Code: Andor Czafik) natively to Pebble Time, Pebble Time Round, Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.

🖍️ Cartoon Rainbow Letters: The original Kiddo digits and letters with a black outline and a 3D shadow. Every letter can get its own random color (default), one random color per text, or one fixed color.
🎨 Always Vivid Colors: The background and the health board always use vivid Pebble colors, and the letters, numbers and icons always clearly differ from what is behind them.
🌈 Background Color Modes:
  Choose one color.
  Random every day.
  Random every hour.
  Random every minute (default).
  Random on wrist flick.
🪵 Health Board: 3 + 3 configurable slots. Pick from Heart rate (Pebble Time 2), Steps, Active minutes, Distance (km or miles), Calories, Sleep hours, the Vibration bell, or Nothing.
🔔 Quiet Time Indicator: Shown by default. A bell with ON while the watch may vibrate, a crossed bell with OFF during Quiet Time.
👋 Wrist Flick Second Board: Flick your wrist to show the second board for 20 seconds, with an optional short vibration that respects Quiet Time.
❤️ On-Demand Heart Rate (Pebble Time 2): The optional "Measure heart rate on wrist flick" mode requests one reading per second for up to 45 seconds, stopping once two consistent readings confirm your BPM. The heart blinks while measuring.
🔋 Battery: The battery icon color follows the charge level, and a + appears while charging.
⌚ Round Displays: Dedicated layouts for Pebble Time Round (180x180) and Pebble Round 2 (260x260), with the weekday and AM/PM pulled in from the edge.
⚙️ Battery Friendly Offline Settings: Updates once a minute. Configure it entirely offline via Clay in the Pebble app.

🧡 Kiddo is completely free. If you enjoy it, you can support our work here:
https://czandor.hu/paypal/kiddo
```
